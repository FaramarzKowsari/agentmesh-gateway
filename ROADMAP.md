# Roadmap

## v0.1 foundation

Typed protocol ingress, two provider families, five routing policies, retry/fallback, circuit breaking, authentication, model catalog, administration, and Responses text streaming.

## Future candidates

- Native streaming pass-through for chat and Anthropic messages
- Tool calls, images, structured output, and richer usage accounting
- Persistent/distributed health and adaptive latency measurement
- Graceful draining, rate limits, audit events, and OpenTelemetry opt-in hooks
- Provider capability discovery and broader protocol conformance suites

Items are intentions, not commitments.
